About Me

I am an Information Systems student at Universitas Negeri Semarang with a deep interest in modern technology. Currently, I am evolving into a Fullstack Developer, focusing on building responsive and functional web applications. I enjoy the process of turning complex ideas into clean, user-friendly digital solutions using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the Bootstrap framework.

Beyond web development, I have a strong passion for Robotics and Automation. I am experienced in hardware programming using C++, specifically for Arduino and Ardupilot systems. I love exploring the synergy between software and hardware, building intelligent systems that can interact with the physical world, from autonomous controllers to integrated robotic solutions.
